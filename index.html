Personal tool

AdLoop Hippolyte

AdLoop Hippolyte is a personal assistant that helps Hippolyte, a Google Ads Search specialist, analyse the Google Ads, Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and Google Search Console data of the accounts he is authorised to access.

What it does

  • Reads Google Ads reports (campaigns, keywords, search terms, ads) to audit accounts.
  • Uses the Google Ads Keyword Planner to estimate search volumes and costs before a campaign is built.
  • Reads Google Analytics, Tag Manager and Search Console data to check conversion tracking and organic visibility.
  • Any change to an advertising account is drafted, previewed and validated by a human before it is applied. Budgets are never changed without explicit written approval.

Who can use it

This application is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from other users.

Privacy. Read how Google user data is accessed, used and protected in the Privacy Policy.
